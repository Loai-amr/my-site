Why this is a revenue number, not a vanity metric

Google's mobile UX research puts bounce probability at roughly +90% when load time goes from 1s to 5s. Applying an 80% drop-off at the 2-second mark — a number any business owner can check against their own analytics — to a site running at 16 seconds means most traffic never saw the page at all.

// back-of-envelope, using the business's own numbers

$ 1,000 monthly visits, 80% leave by second 2

→ at 16s: ~800 of 1,000 visits bounce before the page is usable

→ at 1.2s: those 800 visits become reachable

$ even a modest 3% conversion on the newly-reachable traffic

→ ~24 additional leads/month, previously invisible to the funnel

// illustrative math based on a common industry bounce assumption — I'd always validate against real analytics before quoting this to a client. The same logic applies directly to Shopify checkout: every extra second is cart abandonment.
